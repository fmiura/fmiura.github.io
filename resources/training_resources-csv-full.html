Training resources

Curated courses and learning materials for infectious disease modelling, epidemiological statistics, mathematical epidemiology, stochastic processes, and network science.

This page lists selected courses, summer schools, online materials, textbooks, and tutorials for infectious disease modelling, epidemiological statistics, mathematical epidemiology, stochastic processes, and network science.

The list is manually curated. Course dates, fees, eligibility, and application deadlines may change; always check the official page. The last_checked field records when I last reviewed each entry.

Suggested routes

These routes are subjective and intended as quick orientation rather than a formal curriculum.

Goal Suggested resources
Regression and epidemiological statistics Imperial/Coursera public-health R specialization; Nahhas book; Wageningen Advanced Analytical Epidemiology
Basic infectious disease modelling FUN-MOOC or Imperial/Coursera Infectious Disease Modelling; LSHTM introduction course
Implementing transmission models LSHTM Modern Techniques in Infectious Disease Modelling
Fitting infectious disease dynamic models Statistical Rethinking; LSHTM Model Fitting and Inference
Mathematical epidemiology Diekmann, Heesterbeek & Britton; Keeling & Rohani
Stochastic epidemic models ESPIDAM stochastic epidemic models course; Allen primer; Britton notes
Network and contact-pattern modelling Utrecht Network Science; ESPIDAM Networks and contact patterns; SISMID Network Modeling for Epidemics
Stochastic-process foundations MIT OCW Introduction to Stochastic Processes; MITx Probability
