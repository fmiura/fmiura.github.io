Training resources

Curated courses and learning materials for infectious disease modelling, epidemiological statistics, mathematical epidemiology, stochastic processes, and network science.

This page collects selected courses, summer schools, online materials, textbooks, and tutorials for infectious disease modelling and adjacent methods.

The list is manually curated. Course dates, fees, eligibility, and application deadlines may change; always check the official page. The last_checked field records when each entry was last reviewed.

Data and maintenance

The source of truth is data/training_resources.csv. To update the page, edit the CSV and render the site again.

Suggested maintenance rules:

  • keep one resource per row;
  • update last_checked whenever an entry is reviewed;
  • use official course pages as URLs whenever possible;
  • keep notes short;
  • do not delete past courses immediately if they are likely to recur;
  • use status = unknown when the current status is unclear.

Inclusion in this list does not imply endorsement by any affiliated institution.
